# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Uploads as downloaded:** upload the export .zip (or file) without unzipping or picking the network; Bot Purge works out which network or message type it is. Drag and drop onto the upload boxes. Picking it yourself still works.
- **Yahoo Mail:** inbox connection, next to Gmail and Outlook. All three now check the Inbox and Spam folders.
- **Email cleanup (optional):** with the extra permission, move every flagged sender's emails to Trash or Spam in one press. Never deleted for good.
- **Your agent tab (optional extras):**
  - Teach the agent when it doesn't know how: write the steps, show it in its window, or talk it through, for removing, blocking, reporting, unfriending and live-chat moderation.
  - Talk to your agent to set what to scan ("only my TikTok followers for bots, spam and fraud"), and to fine-tune its pace, limits, default actions, trusted accounts, rescans, live-chat rules and objectives.
  - Optional saved sign-in in the desktop app; codes and puzzles are handed to you.

## 1.0.0

- **Scan:** friends, followers and following on X, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn, with antivirus-style threat counts and plain-language reasons.
- **Choose what to do:** remove, block or report, with one-click (X), assisted, guided or done-for-you modes, and an undo log.
- **Messages:** DMs, comments, live chat, texts (Android and iPhone) and email, plus live Gmail, Outlook and Yahoo Mail connections (read-only).
- **Connected apps:** risky follower-growth, auto-like and "who viewed my profile" apps.
- **Canary traps:** expose AI comment bots.
- **Live Guard:** removes bots from live chats as they appear (Twitch and YouTube APIs; moderator account elsewhere).
- **Done-for-you agent:** understands keyboard shortcuts, multi-screen flows, pop-ups and new windows.
- **Plans:** Cleanup ($20 once) and Protect ($60/month), with Stripe checkout and signed license keys. Every plan is free during the beta.
- **Platform Purge Console:** tiers, dry runs, rollback, appeals and a tamper-evident audit log, with Discord, Discourse, Shopify and WordPress adapters.
- **Downloads and phones:** desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux with an update check, and an installable phone app.
