    Connected apps

    Follower-growth, auto-like and "who viewed my profile" apps are bots acting through your account. Upload the connected-apps file from your data export: X connected-application.js, or Facebook/Instagram "Apps and websites".

    Canary traps: catch AI comment bots

    AI bots read your post and follow any instruction in it. People skip nonsense. Put a canary instruction in your caption, a pinned comment or your video, then paste the comments here. Anyone who repeats the phrase is a confirmed AI bot. Bot Purge never posts or uploads anything for you.

    Live Guard: bots removed from your live chat as they appear

    Bot Purge moderates your stream's chat: fake giveaways ("first 10 to type WIN get $10,000"), "check my bio" bots, crypto doubling, impersonators of you or your mods, floods and scripted raids. It never touches you, your moderators, VIPs, subscribers or people you trust. It judges behaviour, never opinions. Start in Watch mode to see what it would do, then switch to Protect.

    Connect your moderator account (Twitch / YouTube)

    Twitch: make a moderator account (or use your own), create a token with moderator:manage:banned_users, moderator:manage:chat_messages and chat:read. YouTube: an OAuth token for the channel owner or a moderator with the youtube.force-ssl scope; the channel is the live chat ID.
